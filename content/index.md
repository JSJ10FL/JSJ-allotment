### A digital pick-your-own for growing healthy churches

Come on in, simply because you are curious, or because you have a question that you want to take further.    
Fill your trug with as much as you can cook, or as much as you as those around you can eat.    
You will find several different beds, where you can help yourself to whatever looks tasty.  
There is a bench at centre, where you can pray before you dive in.  Check out the growing guides, and some recipes from earlier years and from other cultures.  
Cook, chew, enjoy, feast together, or plant, tend and harvest later.  
Enjoy.
